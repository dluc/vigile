## What it answers

The recorded decision's ID, title, explicit state and version.

## Rules

A decision needs no task or container. Choose `proposed` or `active` at creation. A project may supply the ID prefix without creating an impact link.

## Errors

`invalid_input` means a required field is missing or invalid. `not_found` means the project does not exist.
